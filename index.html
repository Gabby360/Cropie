Agronomic Reasoning Breakdown for Ejura Maize (Vegetative Stage):
  • Field Inspection (Recommended): Rain probability at 72% with 78% humidity during early vegetative stage creates ideal microclimate for Fall Armyworm larva emergence. Checking leaves today prevents infestation.
  • Fertilizer Application (Review Timing): High 72% rain probability in the afternoon means applied granular N-P-K fertilizer will likely wash away before root absorption. Postpone until tomorrow morning.
  • Spraying (Check Conditions): Rain within 3-4 hours of chemical application strips crop protection product, rendering it ineffective and contaminating runoff.
